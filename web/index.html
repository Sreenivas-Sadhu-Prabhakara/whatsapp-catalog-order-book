Food & small retail · India & the Philippines

Product catalogue and order book for a small shop

Your catalogue and your orders, on your own machine, adding themselves up. Every order keeps the price it was taken at, every day gets one list of what to make, and every message a customer needs comes out already written — with a prefilled chat link beside it. This tool sends nothing itself. You read the message and send it, from your own phone.
